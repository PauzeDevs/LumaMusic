Privacy policy.

LAST UPDATED: 7 NOVEMBER 2026
This policy describes the current LumaMusic app and website. The app is an early-stage project. Features and data practices may change; this page must be revised before introducing accounts, analytics, advertising, or payments.

1. Who operates LumaMusic

LumaMusic is an independent software project developed by PauzeDevs. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Spotify, Apple, or Google/YouTube.

2. Information stored on your device

The current app can store your liked tracks, recently played history, local playlists, and playback preferences on your device using local application storage. These items are used to provide the library and player experience. They are not currently associated with a LumaMusic user account or a cloud-sync service.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its app data may remove locally stored information. The project does not currently provide an account-based export or recovery feature.

3. Music discovery and third-party services

When you browse, search, or stream, the app makes requests to YouTube Music web search endpoints. Those services may receive technical information such as your IP address, request details, and device/network metadata as part of normal internet communication. Google and YouTube process information under their own policies and terms. Review Google's and YouTube's applicable privacy documentation for details.

The website loads web fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser may contact Google to retrieve those fonts. You can block external font requests through browser settings or network controls; the site will fall back to system fonts.

4. Information we do not currently ask for

The current project does not provide LumaMusic sign-in, a payment flow, targeted advertising, or a cloud profile. We do not ask you to submit your real name, phone number, contacts, or precise location to use the current core player. Do not send private information in public bug reports or repository issues.

5. Permissions

Audio playback uses the device's media capabilities. The app configuration does not request microphone recording. Operating-system permission prompts, if added in a future version, will be explained in an updated policy.

6. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored library and history data remain on the device until removed by app controls (where available), app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Third-party service logs are controlled by those providers and their retention policies.

7. Children

LumaMusic is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been submitted to the project through a channel we control, contact the project maintainers through the repository so the concern can be reviewed.

8. Security and changes

No software or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. We work to keep the project and its dependencies maintained, but cannot promise uninterrupted or risk-free operation. This policy may change as the app evolves. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, use the LumaMusic GitHub issue tracker. Do not post sensitive personal data publicly.
