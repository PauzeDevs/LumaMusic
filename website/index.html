01 / IMPLEMENTED FEATURES

What the app
actually does.

A feature list based on the current application code. No concept screens or sample data.

01

DISCOVERY

Browse and search YouTube Music

Home displays trending tracks and trending playlists. Search can return tracks, artists and playlists from YouTube Music.

02

PLAYBACK

Control the current track

Play, pause, skip forward or back, and seek through a track using the in-app progress control.

03

QUEUE

Manage what plays next

Open Up Next, move queued tracks, remove upcoming tracks, or clear the queue while keeping the current track.

04

PERSONAL LIBRARY

Keep your saved music local

Like tracks, create personal playlists and revisit recently played tracks. Library data is persisted on the device.

05

PLAYBACK OPTIONS

Adjust the listening session

Choose playback speed from 0.75× to 2×, enable shuffle or repeat, and set a timed pause or end-of-track sleep timer.

06

ARTISTS & PLAYLISTS

Open artist and playlist pages

View artist information and available tracks, or open supported playlist details and play tracks from those pages.

Background audio and lock-screen controls are configured in the app. Behaviour can vary by operating system and device and still requires release-device testing.

02 / MUSIC CATALOGUE

Powered by
YouTube Music.

LumaMusic currently uses YouTube Music for discovery, track metadata and eligible audio streams. Search availability depends on YouTube Music and may change.

LumaMusic does not provide the full Spotify or Apple Music catalogue, and does not scrape or extract audio from those services. A track being available on another platform does not mean it will be available here.
