03 / THE MUSIC CATALOGUE

Discovery starts
with YouTube Music.

Luma Music uses an unofficial YouTube Music web search interface for track discovery and metadata. When you choose a result, the official YouTube Music app or website handles playback.

It does not mirror Spotify or Apple Music catalogues, extract audio, or host music. Availability and service behaviour depend on third-party platforms.
